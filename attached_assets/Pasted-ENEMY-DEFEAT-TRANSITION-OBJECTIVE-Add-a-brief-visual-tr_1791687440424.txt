ENEMY DEFEAT TRANSITION

OBJECTIVE

Add a brief visual transition between the final blow and the defeat screen.

Currently, when an enemy reaches 0 Stamina, the defeat screen appears immediately.

This makes combat endings feel abrupt.

The player should have a moment to register that the enemy has been defeated before the defeat dialogue appears.

====================================================

NEW FLOW

Current:

Enemy reaches 0 Stamina
→ Defeat Screen appears immediately

----------------------------------------------------

Desired:

Enemy reaches 0 Stamina
→ Enemy Defeat Animation
→ Brief Pause
→ Defeat Screen

====================================================

DEFEAT ANIMATION

Use the existing enemy portrait.

Do not create new artwork.

Do not create separate death sprites.

Keep implementation lightweight.

====================================================

VISUAL EFFECTS

When an enemy reaches 0 Stamina:

- Fade the enemy portrait.
- Darken the portrait slightly.
- Fade the enemy name.
- Fade the enemy stamina bar.

The enemy should feel as though it is leaving the scene rather than simply disappearing.

Avoid dramatic effects.

Avoid excessive flashing.

Avoid screen-wide effects.

====================================================

TIMING

Target duration:

2.5 seconds

The transition should feel deliberate and atmospheric.

The player should have enough time to recognize that the enemy has been defeated.

The transition should not feel rushed.

====================================================

OPTIONAL ENHANCEMENTS

If simple to implement:

- Add a subtle drift of smoke or shadow as the portrait fades.
- Add a gentle vignette during the transition.
- Add a brief lingering pause once the portrait is almost gone.

These effects should remain understated.

====================================================

LORD BLACKTHORN EXCEPTION

Do not apply the normal defeat transition when Lord Blackthorn Phase 1 reaches 0 Stamina.

Lord Blackthorn Phase 1 does not end the encounter.

Instead:

Lord Blackthorn Phase 1 reaches 0 Stamina
→ Existing transformation sequence
→ Lord Blackthorn Phase 2 begins

Maintain the current transformation flow.

Do not display:

- Defeat screen
- Defeat dialogue
- Victory transition

between Phase 1 and Phase 2.

The normal defeat transition should only occur when:

- Butler is defeated
- Hanging Lady is defeated
- Shadow Hound is defeated
- Bat Swarm is defeated
- Lord Blackthorn Phase 2 is defeated

Only the final defeat of Lord Blackthorn Phase 2 should trigger the normal enemy defeat transition and final defeat screen.

====================================================

DEFEAT SCREEN

After the transition completes:

Display the normal defeat screen and defeat dialogue.

Do not change:

- Defeat dialogue
- Combat balance
- Rewards
- Progression

====================================================

DESIGN GOAL

The player should feel:

"I defeated the enemy."

before seeing:

"The enemy is gone."

The defeat screen should feel like the consequence of victory rather than an instant popup replacement.

The transition should reinforce the atmospheric, gothic tone of Blackthorn Manor while remaining simple and lightweight.
