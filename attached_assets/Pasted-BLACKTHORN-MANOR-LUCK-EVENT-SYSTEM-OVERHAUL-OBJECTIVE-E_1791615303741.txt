BLACKTHORN MANOR
LUCK EVENT SYSTEM OVERHAUL

OBJECTIVE

Expand Luck into a core gameplay system.

Luck should no longer feel like a combat-only statistic.

Luck should influence discovery, exploration, hidden rooms, environmental hazards, supernatural encounters, special item finds, and critical moments.

Luck should create memorable stories and make the Manor feel alive and unpredictable.

The player should often feel:

"I got lucky."

or

"The Manor nearly got me."

====================================================
DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
====================================================

Luck should create moments.

Luck should create stories.

Luck should create tension.

Luck should occasionally save the player.

Luck should occasionally punish the player.

Luck should not be a simple percentage modifier shown to the player.

Luck should feel mysterious.

Luck should feel like fate.

====================================================
LUCK EVENT TYPES
====================================================

Luck may be used for:

- Hidden discoveries
- Secret passages
- False walls
- Concealed compartments
- Hidden Family Fragments
- Potion discoveries
- Special item discoveries
- Environmental hazards
- Supernatural encounters
- Critical combat opportunities

====================================================
LOCATION-AWARE LUCK EVENTS
====================================================

Luck events must make sense for the room where they occur.

Do NOT assign Luck events randomly.

The room should determine the type of Luck event.

Players should never think:

"Why would that happen here?"

Instead they should think:

"That makes sense."

====================================================
ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS
====================================================

The Manor should occasionally threaten the player.

Examples:

- Falling portrait
- Falling chandelier
- Collapsing shelf
- Falling cabinet contents
- Rolling boulder
- Weak stair
- Swinging iron decoration

====================================================
EXAMPLE: FALLING PORTRAIT
====================================================

A portrait begins to tilt.

Test Luck.

Success:

You instinctively move aside.

The frame crashes to the floor.

Failure:

The frame strikes your shoulder.

Lose 1 Stamina.

====================================================
EXAMPLE: FALLING CHANDELIER
====================================================

A chain snaps overhead.

Test Luck.

Success:

You leap clear.

The chandelier explodes across the floor.

Failure:

Falling debris strikes you.

Lose 2 Stamina.

Gain 1 Fear.

====================================================
EXAMPLE: ROLLING BOULDER
====================================================

A distant rumble echoes through the passage.

Something is accelerating toward you.

Test Luck.

Success:

You dive into a narrow niche.

A massive stone crashes past.

Failure:

The boulder catches you before you can react.

Lose 2 Stamina.

Gain 1 Fear.

====================================================
SUPERNATURAL EVENTS
====================================================

Luck should occasionally determine how strongly the player is affected by the Manor's supernatural activity.

Success Example:

The whisper fades before you can hear it clearly.

No Fear gained.

Failure Example:

The whisper reaches your ear.

Gain 1 Fear.

====================================================
COMBAT INTEGRATION
====================================================

Luck should primarily function passively during combat.

Players should not repeatedly click a Luck button during standard combat.

Luck should automatically influence:

- Critical hit chance
- Critical dodge chance
- Special combat opportunities
- Favorable random outcomes

====================================================
PASSIVE COMBAT EVENTS
====================================================

Lucky Strike

A brief opening appears.

Bonus damage inflicted.

----------------------------------------------------

Lucky Dodge

The attack narrowly misses.

No damage taken.

----------------------------------------------------

Lucky Opportunity

An enemy weakness becomes obvious.

Bonus damage opportunity unlocked.

====================================================
HOUSE SCORE REWARDS
====================================================

Certain successful Luck discoveries should reward:

+1 House Score

Particularly when the player uncovers:

- Manor secrets
- Hidden lore
- Family history
- Secret rooms
- Family Fragments

====================================================
FEAR INTERACTION
====================================================

Fear and Luck should influence one another.

At higher Fear levels:

- Discoveries may become harder
- Hazards may become more dangerous
- Supernatural encounters may have stronger consequences

Do not expose calculations.

This should happen behind the scenes.

====================================================
EVENT FREQUENCY
====================================================

Luck events should feel special.

Do not place Luck events in every room.

Use them sparingly.

The player should never know exactly when Luck might matter.

====================================================
DESIGN GOAL
====================================================

Players should leave Blackthorn Manor remembering moments such as:

"I discovered a hidden passage."

"I narrowly avoided a falling chandelier."

"A portrait almost crushed me."

"I found a potion behind a bookshelf."

"I escaped a rolling boulder."

"The Manor tried to kill me."

Luck should help make Blackthorn Manor feel alive.

The Manor should sometimes reward curiosity.

The Manor should sometimes punish carelessness.

Most importantly:

The Manor itself should feel like a character.
