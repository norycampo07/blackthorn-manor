INVESTIGATION COMPLETION PHILOSOPHY ALIGNMENT

After reviewing the current implementation, I would like to align the behavior of the "Already explored — view discovery" indicator with the long-term design direction of Blackthorn Manor.

==================================================
CURRENT UNDERSTANDING
==================================================

The eye icon and "Already explored — view discovery" status are currently driven by:

game.discoveries.includes(choice.id)

The Desk investigation becomes explored because the hidden compartment hotspot explicitly completes:

library-diary

The Bookshelf investigation currently does not become explored because no hotspot completes:

library-bookshelves

==================================================
DESIGN PRINCIPLE
==================================================

An action should become:

"Already explored"

when the player has obtained the action's primary intended outcome.

This should apply consistently across the game.

Not just investigations.

Not just room actions.

All actions.

==================================================
IMPORTANT
==================================================

An action should NOT become explored simply because:

- the player entered the investigation
- the player clicked every hotspot
- the player exhausted all available interactions
- the player collected every optional reward

The eye icon should not represent completionism.

The eye icon should represent meaningful progression.

==================================================
PRIMARY OUTCOME
==================================================

Every action should have one primary outcome.

Examples:

- Item
- Discovery
- Progression Trigger
- Family Fragment
- Encounter
- Future equivalent systems

Once the primary outcome is obtained, the action may be considered explored.

==================================================
LIBRARY EXAMPLES
==================================================

Inspect the Desk

Primary Outcome:

Ancient Diary

Reason:

This is the critical discovery associated with this investigation.

The current explored behavior is acceptable.

==================================================

Browse the Bookshelves

Primary Outcome:

Distant Note

NOT:

Burned Journal

Reason:

Burned Journal is valuable lore and should remain discoverable.

However, Distant Note is the discovery that advances the Manor's underlying mystery and future progression path.

Future content will connect this discovery to the Music Room.

Distant Note is therefore the intended completion trigger for the Bookshelf investigation.

==================================================
DISTANT NOTE
==================================================

When Distant Note is discovered and accepted:

- library-bookshelves should be considered explored
- the eye icon should appear
- "Already explored — view discovery" should display

This mirrors the role Ancient Diary performs for the Desk investigation.

==================================================
LOCKED ACTIONS
==================================================

The existing locked-action behavior is working very well.

Example:

Nursery
→ Wind the music box

Displays:

- lock/key icon
- contextual explanation
- required item clue

without revealing too much.

Please preserve this design approach for future content.

The lock icon should continue representing:

"A prerequisite exists but has not yet been satisfied."

The current implementation feels intuitive and consistent with the Manor's discovery-focused design.

No architecture changes are requested here.

This is simply positive feedback and direction to maintain the same pattern in future rooms, investigations, and progression chains.

==================================================
LONG-TERM DESIGN DIRECTION
==================================================

Supporting content:

- Investigation Notes
- Family Fragments
- Optional Items
- Flavor Interactions

should enrich the experience.

They should not automatically determine whether an investigation is explored.

Instead, each investigation should contain one meaningful discovery that represents:

"I found the important secret here."

That discovery should control investigation completion.

==================================================
GOAL
==================================================

The eye icon should communicate:

"I have obtained the primary outcome of this action."

NOT:

"I clicked everything."

The lock icon should communicate:

"I do not yet possess the required prerequisite."

Together these indicators create a simple and consistent language that scales across:

- Room actions
- Investigations
- Future Manor expansions
- Future progression chains
