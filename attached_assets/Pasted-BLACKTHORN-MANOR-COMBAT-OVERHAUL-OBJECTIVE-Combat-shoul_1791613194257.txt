BLACKTHORN MANOR
COMBAT OVERHAUL

OBJECTIVE

Combat should feel like a scene from a gothic adventure novel rather than a sequence of dice rolls.

The goal is to make combat:

- More atmospheric
- More readable
- More cinematic
- Less repetitive
- More story-driven

No major balance changes are required.

====================================================
ENEMY STAMINA DISPLAY
====================================================

Display enemy stamina during combat.

Players should always know how close an enemy is to defeat.

Display:

Enemy Name

Enemy Stamina Bar

Current Enemy Stamina

Example:

THE BUTLER

███████░░░

7 / 10

Do not hide enemy stamina.

Combat progress should always be visible.

====================================================
COMBAT RESULT CLARITY
====================================================

Combat results should be immediately understandable.

Avoid forcing players to interpret dice values.

Examples:

YOU WIN THE EXCHANGE

THE BUTLER WINS THE EXCHANGE

CRITICAL STRIKE

-2 STAMINA

-1 FEAR

Provide clear feedback after each combat exchange.

====================================================
CONTINUOUS COMBAT FLOW
====================================================

Current combat requires repeatedly clicking Attack.

Reduce click fatigue.

New behavior:

The player clicks:

Attack

Combat enters a continuous exchange mode.

The game automatically resolves combat exchanges back and forth.

Example:

You attack.

The Butler defends.

The Butler attacks.

You dodge.

You strike.

The combat continues automatically.

====================================================

Continuous combat should pause when:

- Enemy dialogue triggers
- Major combat events occur
- The player wants to use an item
- The player wants to use a potion
- An enemy reaches a dialogue threshold
- Fear events occur
- The battle ends

When a pause occurs:

Display:

Continue Battle

The player resumes combat by clicking once.

====================================================
ENEMY PERSONALITY SYSTEM
====================================================

Combat should be a storytelling system.

Enemies should occasionally speak during battle.

Dialogue should be short.

Dialogue should be memorable.

Dialogue should reveal personality, threat, or story.

Avoid long speeches.

Prefer:

- One-line taunts
- One-line warnings
- One-line revelations

====================================================
DIALOGUE THRESHOLDS
====================================================

Enemies should have unique dialogue at:

- Battle Start
- Approximately 50% Stamina
- Approximately 25% Stamina
- Defeat

====================================================
SHADOW HOUND
====================================================

The Shadow Hound should NOT speak.

No human language.

No telepathy.

No dialogue.

Use descriptive combat text instead.

Examples:

"A low growl emerges from the darkness."

"The creature circles beyond the lantern light."

"The Hound snaps at your throat."

"The beast retreats a step."

"The darkness behind it does not."

====================================================
BAT SWARM
====================================================

Initially the Bat Swarm appears to be only bats.

The player should gradually realize something else is present.

Battle Start:

"Hundreds of wings erupt from the darkness."

50% Stamina:

"The swarm briefly forms an unnatural shape."

25% Stamina:

"A voice seems to whisper through the wings."

Defeat:

"The swarm collapses.

For a brief moment, a human silhouette appears.

Then vanishes."

====================================================

====================================================

Combat should occasionally include atmospheric observations.

Nobody remembers winding it."

"The candle flame bends against a wind that should not exist."

"The portraits seem closer than before."

"The darkness shifts."

"The Manor listens."

These should appear between combat exchanges.

====================================================
LUCK SYSTEM
====================================================

Luck should become primarily passive.

Remove the need for frequent Luck button usage during combat.

Luck should instead influence:

- Critical hit chance
- Successful dodges
- Favorable random outcomes
- Event success rates

The goal is fewer clicks and smoother combat flow.

====================================================
DESIGN GOAL
====================================================

Combat should feel like fighting characters, not statistics.

Enemies should reveal personality and story throughout battle.

The player should feel they are surviving encounters within Blackthorn Manor itself.

Combat should remain simple, fast, atmospheric, and story-driven.
