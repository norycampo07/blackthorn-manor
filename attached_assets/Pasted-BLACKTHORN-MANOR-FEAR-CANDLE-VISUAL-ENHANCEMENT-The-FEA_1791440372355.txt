BLACKTHORN MANOR
FEAR CANDLE VISUAL ENHANCEMENT

The FEAR meter currently uses tall, pristine candles.

The candles appear newly lit.

This weakens the narrative of the Fear system.

DESIGN GOAL

The candles should feel as though they have been burning throughout the player's journey.

They are the last lights standing against the darkness.

The player should feel that each candle is fragile and temporary.

VISUAL CHANGES

Replace the current tall candle design with shorter, partially melted candles.

Requirements:

- Uneven candle heights
- Visible melted wax
- Wax drips running down the sides
- Slightly crooked or imperfect silhouettes
- Darkened wicks
- Small pools of hardened wax at the base

The candles should look old and worn.

Not decorative.

Not ceremonial.

ANIMATION IMPROVEMENTS

The flame animation should feel alive and fragile.

Requirements:

- Gentle flickering
- Slight variation between candles
- Occasional brief dimming
- Small shifts in flame size
- Subtle glow pulses

Avoid:

- Uniform movement
- Perfect synchronization
- Bright cartoon flames

FEAR LOSS VISUALIZATION

When Fear increases and a candle is lost:

- Flame extinguishes
- Thin smoke trail rises briefly
- Wick glows faintly before fading
- Candle remains visible

Do not remove the candle completely.

The remains should stay visible.

The player should see evidence of lost light.

EXTINGUISHED CANDLES

Extinguished candles should appear:

- Partially melted
- Darkened wick
- Thin smoke residue
- Faint wax remains

They should feel like lights that recently died.

HIGH FEAR ATMOSPHERE

As Fear grows:

- Remaining flames flicker more violently
- Overall candle glow weakens
- The FEAR panel becomes slightly darker
- Visual tension increases

LOW FEAR ATMOSPHERE

When many candles remain:

- Flames burn steadier
- Warm glow is stronger
- The panel feels safer

NARRATIVE GOAL

The FEAR meter should not feel like a statistic.

It should feel like ten fragile lights protecting the player from the darkness.

Every extinguished candle should feel like a small loss.

The player should worry when only one candle remains.
