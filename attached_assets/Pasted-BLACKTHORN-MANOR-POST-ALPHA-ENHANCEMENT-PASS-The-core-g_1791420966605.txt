BLACKTHORN MANOR
POST-ALPHA ENHANCEMENT PASS

The core game is working.

Do NOT redesign the game from scratch.

Preserve:

- Exploration
- Fear system
- House Whispers
- Combat
- Inventory
- Map
- Family Fragments
- Multiple endings

Focus on atmosphere, artwork integration, discovery, usability, and polish.

1. INTERFACE ORGANIZATION

LEFT SIDEBAR = STATUS

Contains:

- Player Profile
- Stamina
- Skill
- Luck
- Fear
- House Score
- Carried
- Clues
- Achievements

Answers:

"How am I doing?"

CENTER PANEL = GAMEPLAY

Contains:

- Room Artwork
- Room Description
- Exploration
- Discoveries
- Choices
- Combat
- Events
- Ending Sequences

Answers:

"What is happening?"

RIGHT SIDEBAR = INFORMATION

Contains:

- Minimap
- Inventory
- Family Fragments
- Whispers

Answers:

"What do I know?"

2. MINIMAP IMPROVEMENT

Keep the minimap in the right sidebar.

Move it lower so it aligns visually with the player choice area.

Requirements:

- Sticky position while scrolling
- Always visible beside room choices
- Slightly larger size
- Hover room names
- Current room clearly highlighted
- Preserve "Open Full Map"

The map is a navigation tool, not a dashboard widget.

3. WHISPER SYSTEM CONSOLIDATION

Remove from room content:

- House Whispers block
- A Voice In The Wall block

Retain:

- Whisper panel in right sidebar

The Whisper panel becomes the single source of supernatural communication.

4. LISTEN TO THE WALLS REWORK

When the player clicks:

"Listen To The Walls"

Do not inject text into room content.

Instead:

- Generate a Whisper
- Update Whisper panel
- Highlight Whisper panel briefly
- Increment Whisper count

Example:

WHISPER 4/10

"The faithful buried more than their dead."

10. INVENTORY USABILITY IMPROVEMENT

Players cannot easily determine which items have already been used.

Add item states.

ACTIVE

Items still usable.

Display normally.

USED

Items which completed their purpose.

Do not remove them.

Keep them as part of the player's story.

Examples:

Brass Key
→ Broken Key icon

Rope
→ Frayed Rope icon

Matchbox
→ Empty Matchbox icon

Lantern
→ Extinguished Lantern icon

Silver Crucifix
→ Tarnished Crucifix icon

Display item names in slightly faded text.

Display:

USED

below their description.

CONSUMABLES

Show remaining uses.

Examples:

Matchbox

3 matches remain

Rusty Revolver

1 round remains

LEGENDARY ITEMS

Never appear as used.

Always remain visually prominent.

Examples:

- Blackthorn Dagger
- Red Gem
- Family Seal Ring
- Ancient Diary

Keep unique icons and full brightness.

Design goal:

The inventory should tell the story of the player's journey.

15. DESIGN GOAL

Blackthorn Manor should feel like:

- A digital gothic gamebook
- An interactive mystery
- A haunted inheritance story

The player should remember:

- The House
- The Butler
- The Blackthorn Dagger
- Lord Blackthorn
- The final whisper

more than the score itself.

Focus on:

- Mystery
- Discovery
- Atmosphere
- Storytelling

Every image should feel like a revelation.

Every room should feel like a secret.

Every ending should feel like the final page of a gothic novel.
