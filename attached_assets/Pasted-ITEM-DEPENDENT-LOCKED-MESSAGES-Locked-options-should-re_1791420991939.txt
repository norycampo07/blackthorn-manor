ITEM-DEPENDENT LOCKED MESSAGES

Locked options should remain clickable.

When a player clicks a locked option, display an atmospheric message based on the missing item.

The message should depend on the required item, not the room.

This allows the same item to consistently communicate its purpose throughout the game.

DESIGN GOAL

Locked interactions should never feel like a game restriction.

They should feel like a clue, warning, or piece of lore.

Players should leave with:

- A hint about the required item
- A reason to continue exploring
- Additional atmosphere

rather than simply being told an item is missing.
