BLACKTHORN MANOR
FINAL POLISH PASS

OBJECTIVE

Refine combat presentation, Luck event pacing, defeat screens, and result sharing.

These changes focus on atmosphere, clarity, and player experience without introducing new gameplay mechanics.

====================================================
COMBAT DIALOGUE THRESHOLD FIX
====================================================

Current Issue

If an enemy loses multiple health thresholds quickly, especially when exploiting a weakness item, threshold dialogue may never appear during combat.

Instead, missed dialogue is sometimes displayed together on the defeat screen.

Example:

"I tried to leave."

"Do not trust the Manor."

"Thank you."

This creates awkward dialogue and weakens the impact of the final defeat line.

====================================================

DESIRED BEHAVIOR

50% dialogue:
Combat only.

25% dialogue:
Combat only.

Defeat dialogue:
Defeat screen only.

====================================================

If an enemy dies before a threshold dialogue can be shown:

- Skip the missed threshold dialogue.
- Do not queue it.
- Do not display it later.
- Do not append it to the defeat screen.

====================================================

DESIGN GOAL

Fast victories naturally reveal less enemy dialogue.

Longer battles reveal more enemy personality and story.

Only the defeat dialogue should appear after victory.

====================================================

Current Issue

"Test Your Luck"

2. Player clicks:

====================================================
LUCK AND FEAR ANIMATION ORDER
====================================================

If a Luck event causes Fear:

Animation sequence MUST be:

1. Coin Flip Animation

2. Fear Candle Animation

3. Luck Result Window

4. Player clicks Continue

5. Return to gameplay

The coin flip animation and Fear candle animation must never overlap.

The coin flip animation must fully complete before the Fear animation begins.

The Fear animation must fully complete before the Luck Result Window appears.

This preserves dramatic pacing and makes the sequence feel intentional.

====================================================
COMBAT DAMAGE FEEDBACK
====================================================

OBJECTIVE

Make stamina loss visible and satisfying without using intrusive visual effects.

Players should immediately understand when damage occurs.

====================================================

ENEMY DAMAGE FEEDBACK

When an enemy loses stamina:

- Briefly shudder the enemy portrait.
- Duration approximately 100-150ms.
- Keep the movement subtle.

Display:

-1 STAMINA

or

-2 STAMINA

near the enemy stamina bar.

The value should briefly appear and fade.

Optional:

Apply a very subtle dark red tint to the portrait for a brief moment.

Avoid excessive flashing.

====================================================

PLAYER DAMAGE FEEDBACK

When the player loses stamina:

- Briefly pulse the player stamina bar.
- Briefly highlight the updated stamina number.
- Use a subtle warm amber or muted red glow.

Optional:

Apply a very gentle scale pulse to the player status area:

98% → 100%

Avoid:

- Blood effects
- Full-screen flashes
- Heavy screen shake
- Eye-straining visual effects

====================================================

DESIGN GOAL

Damage should feel noticeable.

Players should immediately understand:

"I was hurt."

or

"The enemy was hurt."

while preserving the gothic atmosphere of Blackthorn Manor.
