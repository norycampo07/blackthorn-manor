BLACKTHORN MANOR
FIRST-TIME PLAYER EXPERIENCE OVERHAUL

OBJECTIVE

Improve the onboarding and first-play experience without introducing traditional tutorials.

The game should teach itself through subtle visual guidance.

Players should learn game mechanics naturally through interaction and exploration.

====================================================
ITEM IMAGE INTEGRATION
====================================================

Whenever the player discovers, acquires, examines, or uses an item, display the corresponding item image.

Players should visually encounter inventory artwork during gameplay.

Items should not rely solely on text descriptions.

====================================================
FIRST-TIME PLAYER GUIDANCE
====================================================

Do NOT create a tutorial.

Do NOT create instruction screens.

Do NOT interrupt gameplay.

Guide the player using visual cues only.

Use:

- Soft glow
- Pulse animation
- Subtle highlight

====================================================
FIRST INVESTIGATION
====================================================

At the Front Gate:

Highlight:

"Study the Footprints"

This is the first guided interaction.

If the player clicks:

"Study the Footprints"

then first-time action guidance ends immediately.

No additional action-option guidance should appear anywhere else.

----------------------------------------------------

If the player ignores:

"Study the Footprints"

and moves to the Entrance Hall:

Highlight:

"Look Closely at the Portraits"

Only this option should glow.

----------------------------------------------------

If the player clicks:

"Look Closely at the Portraits"

stop all action-option guidance permanently.

----------------------------------------------------

If the player ignores:

"Look Closely at the Portraits"

then stop all action-option guidance permanently.

The purpose is to nudge the player only once or twice during the opening of the game.

====================================================
MAP DISCOVERY GUIDANCE
====================================================

The Map button should glow until the player opens the map for the first time.

The glow should persist across rooms.

The glow only disappears after the map has been opened.

Optional hover tooltip:

"Interactive Map"

or

"View discovered locations"

Only shown while the glow is active.

====================================================
FIRST ITEM DISCOVERY
====================================================

The first time the player discovers an item:

Highlight:

"Take Item"

The glow remains until the player clicks:

"Take Item"

After the first item has been collected:

Never show this guidance again.

Future items should not glow.

====================================================
FIRST FAMILY FRAGMENT
====================================================

The first time the player discovers a Family Fragment:

Highlight:

"Add to Family Fragments"

The glow remains until the player clicks it.

After the player adds their first fragment:

Never show this guidance again.

Future fragments should not glow.

====================================================
FIRST COMBAT
====================================================

During the first combat encounter only:

Highlight:

"Attack"

Use a subtle red glow.

The glow remains until the player performs their first attack.

Afterwards:

Never show combat guidance again.

====================================================
FEAR SYSTEM OVERHAUL
====================================================

Fear increases should feel like gameplay events.

Do not rely on narrative popups alone.

Whenever Fear increases:

Trigger a brief cinematic Fear animation before the narrative popup appears.

====================================================
FEAR ANIMATION SEQUENCE
====================================================

Total Duration:

1.2 seconds

Sequence:

0.0s - 0.3s

Rapid candle flicker.

The flame becomes unstable.

----------------------------------------------------

0.3s - 0.6s

A sudden gust of unseen wind.

The candle extinguishes.

----------------------------------------------------

0.6s - 0.9s

Thin smoke rises from the wick.

The screen darkens slightly.

----------------------------------------------------

0.9s - 1.2s

Display a large center-screen indicator:

+1 FEAR

or

+2 FEAR

The indicator should be impossible to miss.

====================================================
VISUAL FEAR EFFECTS
====================================================

Combine the candle sequence with:

- Brief screen shudder
- Mild screen flicker
- Soft dark vignette

Effects should feel atmospheric.

Avoid excessive shaking.

Avoid jump-scare effects.

====================================================
NARRATIVE TIMING
====================================================

The Fear animation should complete BEFORE narrative popups are shown.

The player must immediately understand:

1. Something happened.
2. Fear increased.
3. Then the game explains why.

====================================================
DESIGN GOAL
====================================================

The player should learn mechanics through interaction.

The Manor should feel reactive.

Fear should feel meaningful.

Visual guidance should quietly disappear as the player becomes familiar with the game.

The experience should remain immersive and atmospheric without feeling like a tutorial.
