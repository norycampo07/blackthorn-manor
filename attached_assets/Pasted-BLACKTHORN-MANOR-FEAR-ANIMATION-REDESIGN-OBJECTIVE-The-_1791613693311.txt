BLACKTHORN MANOR
FEAR ANIMATION REDESIGN

OBJECTIVE

The current Fear animation is too short and difficult to notice.

The animation feels incomplete because the entire sequence happens too quickly.

The current implementation also uses the Fear gauge candles, which makes the effect visually confusing.

Replace the current Fear animation with a dedicated cinematic Fear sequence.

====================================================
GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
====================================================

Use a single standalone candle.

Do NOT use the existing Fear gauge candles.

Display a single small Victorian candle in the center of the screen.

The candle should:

- Be short
- Look old
- Have dripping wax
- Feel worn and unpleasant
- Match the Blackthorn Manor art style

The candle should exist only for the Fear animation.

====================================================
TOTAL DURATION
====================================================

Total animation duration:

2.5 seconds

The animation should always complete before the narrative popup appears.

====================================================
ANIMATION SEQUENCE
====================================================

PHASE 1
Duration: 0.4 seconds

Show the candle burning normally.

No movement yet.

Purpose:

Give players enough time to notice the candle.

====================================================

PHASE 2
Duration: 0.7 seconds

The candle begins flickering violently.

The flame becomes unstable.

Brightness fluctuates.

Shadows subtly shift.

Players should immediately feel that something is wrong.

====================================================

PHASE 3
Duration: 0.3 seconds

A sudden unseen gust of wind passes.

The flame bends.

The candle extinguishes.

====================================================

PHASE 4
Duration: 0.2 seconds

The flame is gone.

A faint red ember remains briefly.

The screen darkens slightly.

====================================================

PHASE 5
Duration: 0.9 seconds

Smoke rises slowly from the wick.

This should be the longest portion of the animation.

The smoke creates the feeling of dread and loss.

Do not rush this phase.

====================================================

PHASE 6
Duration: 0.5 seconds

Brief pause.

Allow the player to absorb what happened.

No popup yet.

No narrative yet.

====================================================

PHASE 7
Display Fear Gain

Show:

+1 FEAR

or

+2 FEAR

or

+3 FEAR

Large.

Centered.

Impossible to miss.

Fade smoothly.

====================================================
VISUAL EFFECTS
====================================================

During the Fear sequence:

Allow subtle:

- Screen dimming
- Minimal vignette
- Very light screen shudder

Avoid:

- Heavy shaking
- Jump scare effects
- Excessive flashing

The effect should feel atmospheric and unsettling.

====================================================
POPUP TIMING
====================================================

Do NOT display story popups during the candle animation.

The sequence order must be:

1. Fear Event Triggered

2. Candle Animation Plays

3. Fear Gain Displayed

4. Narrative Popup Appears

The player should first experience the event emotionally and then read the explanation.

====================================================
DESIGN GOAL
====================================================

Fear should feel like a meaningful supernatural event.

The player should immediately think:

"Something just happened."

The candle extinguishing should become a recurring visual signature of Blackthorn Manor.

The effect should create dread rather than surprise.
