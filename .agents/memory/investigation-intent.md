---
name: Investigation intent
description: The user's scope and discovery boundaries for focused investigation layers.
---

When an investigation replaces an existing instant discovery, do not add a duplicate room action. Preserve the original relic reward and its source inside the investigation.

**Why:** The user corrected the Library integration: the desk investigation replaces the instant Ancient Diary discovery, rather than adding a fourth choice or removing the diary.

**How to apply:** Keep the room menu's existing action count when replacing a discovery. Move its reward into the appropriate internal investigation step.

Name the room action for the initial inspection, not for a secret the player has not discovered. A hidden compartment must only become apparent through investigating the drawer.

**Why:** The user explicitly said the player should not already know there is a hidden compartment.

**How to apply:** Avoid revealing hidden mechanisms in room-action labels or descriptions. Preserve discovery order inside the focused layer.

Room art provides atmosphere; investigation assets provide interaction. The investigation should feel like leaning closer to an object while still in the same room, not opening a popup, modal, framed window, or separate page.

**Why:** This is the user's stated design philosophy for investigation scenes.

**How to apply:** Dim the whole existing gameplay surface, display the complete transparent investigation asset without a frame, and use Step Back or ESC to return.
