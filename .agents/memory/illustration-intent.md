name: Illustration intent
description: User constraints for room art, creature portraits, and future image interactions
---
The user treats the artwork as environmental storytelling. Preserve the full composition with contain-fit and letterboxing rather than cropping important details or creature heads.

**Why:** Room details are clues, and monster faces, wings, claws, and silhouettes are part of the story.

**How to apply:** Use full-image fitting for rooms and monsters. The user explicitly asked not to implement hotspots during the artwork pass; prepare the architecture only.

Keep persistent inventory and item-use UI text-only. Item artwork belongs in the inventory drawer, examination views, and discovery events. Choosing combat items must not displace the encounter illustration.

**Why:** The user wants room and enemy artwork to remain the primary visual focus, with the sidebar supporting gameplay rather than competing for attention.

**How to apply:** Preserve this hierarchy in future inventory, popup, and responsive layout changes. Map markers must not expose unknown locked interactions or hidden puzzles.
