---
name: Promotional video intent
description: The user's asset, atmosphere, and spoiler boundaries for Blackthorn Manor promotional videos.
---

Promotional videos should use existing game assets, artwork, UI screens, screenshots, and animations wherever possible. Do not generate new artwork or new cinematic scenes.

**Why:** The user requested a lightweight, credit-friendly implementation.

**How to apply:** Reuse the existing game's materials for promotional video work rather than commissioning additional visual assets.

Promotion should encourage curiosity, discovery, dread, and mystery. Do not reveal solutions, endings, hidden room locations, secret encounters, or story twists.

**Why:** The user wants the atmosphere, artwork, and exploration to sell the experience, rather than story spoilers.

**How to apply:** Keep public promotional content spoiler-safe, even when the underlying game assets or UI contain those details.

Keep the Victorian Gothic tone atmospheric, mysterious, and elegant; avoid jump scares, loud horror effects, fast action editing, and excessive visual effects.

**Why:** These are the user's stated promotional style boundaries.

**How to apply:** Preserve restrained motion and sound in revisions as well as new promotional material.
